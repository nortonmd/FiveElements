# Five Elements · Wǔ Xíng

An interactive reference for the five phases in traditional Chinese medicine and their correspondences in Five Animal Frolics, Hung Gar, Sēngmén Five Animal Fists, and Xíngyìquán Five Fists.

[Open the guide](https://nortonmd.github.io/FiveElements/)

Open `index.html` locally or serve this directory with `python3 -m http.server`. The site uses plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. GitHub Pages publishes the root of `main`.

Element associations and lineage references are listed under **Sources and terminology** in the guide. Practice mappings and phase data live in `elements.js`.

## Artwork

The five WebP illustrations in `images/` were created with the built-in image generator. The shared prompt was:

> Stylized-concept, one square illustration for an interactive Five Elements atlas. Full bleed traditional Chinese ink wash with mineral pigment on warm ivory rice paper, visible brush grain, softly faded negative space at corners. The central striking silhouette is [subject]. Closely centered composition fully legible when cropped to a circle and reduced to a small thumbnail. Sophisticated historical natural-history ink painting, atmospheric, strong value contrast. No lettering, no stamp, no symbols, no border.

- Wood — a gnarled pine with vivid moss-green needles rising from a misty cliff, spring haze
- Fire — bright scarlet and vermilion tongues of flame rising from dark charred logs, drifting sparks
- Earth — layered ochre sandstone mesas and a lifted band of rugged soil, warm yellow haze
- Metal — sharp clusters of unpolished silvery-white mineral ore gleaming among dark rock, cool gray haze
- Water — a curling midnight-blue ocean wave with pale foamy brush edges, spray and mist
