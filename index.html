五行

生 Creation

Each phase generates the next. This is the shēng cycle.

克 Destruction

Each phase restrains another. This is the kè cycle, also called the controlling cycle.

Sources and terminology

The organ names refer to traditional zàng (臟) and fǔ (腑) systems. Animal correspondences are specific to the selected practice and can vary between lineages.
